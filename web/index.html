01 / REACTION

Burst Hunt

Tap safe targets, avoid the red X and build a combo before time runs out. Three difficulty levels.

02 / LOGIC PUZZLE

Shift Vault

Swipe pieces across a generated 6×6 board and guide the golden key through the exit.

03 / ONE-TAP RUNNER

Star Pod Sprint

Tap to jump over geometric obstacles, collect coins and keep the pod moving. Three speed modes.

NEW GAME04 / STRATEGY · SOLO OR 2P

Line Four

Drop cyan and pink discs into seven columns. Face the local CPU or share the device with a second player.

NEW GAME05 / MEMORY · SOLO OR 2P

Pair Quest

Flip 16 cards and match eight procedural symbols. Race the clock solo or compete for pairs locally.

NEW GAME06 / NUMBER PUZZLE

Tile Slide

Move numbered tiles into the empty space and restore the sequence on a solvable 3×3 or 4×4 board.

Checking published firmware…

Check your board first. This firmware is only for the Elecrow CrowPanel 2.8-inch ESP32-WROOM-32 (320×240, ILI9341). Not for the ESP32-S3 Rotary Buddy or other CrowPanel models. Chip detection cannot verify the board's screen or pin wiring. If connection fails, try holding BOOT, pressing RESET briefly, then releasing BOOT. Choose “Erase device” only if you intend to clear existing flash data.
